Frequently asked questions

This page is intended to help you find resources. It does not determine individual rights or provide legal advice. Confirm situation-specific questions with official CSKT sources.

Where should I start with water rights questions? +

Start with the information and documents available on this page. If you need help identifying the right resource, contact the Division of Engineering & Water Resources.

Where can I find forms and supporting documents? +

Visit the Forms & Documents and Resources areas of the homepage to browse available materials.

Where can I find maps and GIS resources? +

Use the Maps & GIS resource pathway to find geographic information made available by the department.

Who can I contact for help? +

Contact the CSKT Division of Engineering & Water Resources using the details provided in the site footer.
